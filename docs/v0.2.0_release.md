## Quantified outcomes

Each original scenario has 20 seeds. S01–S04 remain 0/20 collisions. Minimum separation in S02
improves 0.575 → 7.771 m; S03 2.401 → 4.888 m; S04 1.018 → 20.263 m. S04 total retriggers
fall 20 → 0. In the original sweep, mu=0.5 at 60/70/80 km/h improves 20/20 collisions at each
point to 0/20. FI01 and FI02 remain 20/20 collisions; FI02 is now indicated within 120 ms
of command-loss onset. FI01 remains detected 160 ms after last valid sample. Mu=0.3 at
50–80 km/h and speed outside the 80 km/h activation bound still produce collisions.
No conclusion about real-world collision rates follows from these noise-only seed samples.

Earlier braking lowers S03 demand below the emergency-hazard-lamp threshold. Existing lamp
policy and dedicated BCM tests are preserved; the S03 integration expectation was updated
accordingly. Original TTC boundary tests remain, isolated from the new envelope. See audit
for changes to expectations and the safety concept for the increased false-braking tradeoff.
