# Structured engineering assurance case

This educational case study transparently characterizes its specified simulated mechanisms, evidence and unresolved hazards.

**This is not a claim that the vehicle function is safe.** GSN-like notation: C=claim, S=strategy, E=evidence, A=assumption, D=defeater/counterexample. Links are documented arguments, not a formal proof.

Combine reproducibility, mechanism verification, adverse evaluation and explicit defeaters; do not infer acceptable real-world risk.

## C1 — LIMITED

Nominal configured decision thresholds are exercised; universal timely avoidance is not claimed.

Evidence: ecu/tests/test_adas.cpp, results/v1.0.0/final_candidate.json

Defeaters: 29/120 prior hold-outs collide; 47/108 stratified final candidate cases collide

## C2 — SCOPED_SUPPORTED

Configured MAC/authorization/freshness rejection is supported under public-fixture-key assumptions.

Evidence: tests/python/test_v4_security.py, results/v1.0.0/locked_candidate.json

Defeaters: Trusted-key compromise passes dangerous intent; Retained-state total loss permits replay

## C3 — SCOPED_SUPPORTED

Specified detectable sensor/communication/achieved-braking faults are indicated in tested budgets.

Evidence: tests/python/test_v3_faults.py, results/v1.0.0/failure_analysis/review.md

Defeaters: Detection cannot restore missing bandwidth/physical braking

## C4 — SCOPED_SUPPORTED

Newest-authenticated route selection and bounded diagnostic recovery follow tested contracts.

Evidence: tests/python/test_v1_assurance.py, docs/v1.0.0_recovery_decision.md

Defeaters: No aggregate collision reduction in final set; All usable paths stale/lost remain open

## C5 — LIMITED

Specified detectable invalid-input modes have defined native degradation behavior.

Evidence: ecu/src/adas_ecu.cpp, results/v1.0.0/python_test_results.xml

Defeaters: Plausible errors and compromised intent can escape validity checks; Degraded/unavailable can still collide

## C6 — EVALUATION_SUPPORTED

Perception/braking limitations are compared with preserved negative results.

Evidence: results/v1.0.0/perception_study.json, results/v1.0.0/evaluation.md

Defeaters: Temporal/corroboration can cause genuine-obstacle collisions; Common ghosts remain

## C7 — SCOPED_SUPPORTED

Evidence integrity, requirement/test linkage and known lifecycle/model gaps are reviewable.

Evidence: results/v1.0.0/lifecycle_trace.json, results/v1.0.0/sysml-validation.log

Defeaters: PASS is not objective closure; Language validity is not engineering equivalence

## Assumptions and residuals

- Trusted local FFI and shared simulation clock
- Public test keys only
- Declared artificial scenario distributions
- No follower or injury model
- Physical/SOTIF/availability hazards unresolved
- Partial structural coverage and ASan unvalidated
- No ISO/AUTOSAR conformance, homologation or production-readiness claim
